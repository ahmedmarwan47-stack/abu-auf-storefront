The Koueider signature

Our Chocolate Fudge Cake is built on three layers of deeply moist Belgian chocolate sponge, brushed with sugar syrup and generously filled with a velvety chocolate fudge. Every cake is finished by hand with a mirror-glaze ganache and delicate chocolate shavings.

Made fresh to order in our Cairo kitchens using the same family recipe we have perfected since 1961.

  • Three layers of Belgian chocolate sponge
  • Silky chocolate fudge filling
  • Hand-finished ganache glaze
  • No artificial preservatives

What's inside

We use only premium ingredients, sourced fresh every day.

  • Belgian dark chocolate (54% cocoa)
  • Fresh dairy cream and butter
  • Free-range eggs
  • Unbleached wheat flour
  • Natural vanilla and cocoa powder

Allergens: contains gluten, eggs, dairy and may contain traces of nuts.

Delivery & storage

Same-day delivery is available across Greater Cairo for orders placed before 4:00 PM. Nationwide delivery arrives within 1–2 business days in insulated packaging.
